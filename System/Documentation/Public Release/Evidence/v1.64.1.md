## What this evidence does not prove

This evidence qualifies deterministic product mechanics within the recorded scope. It does **not** claim:

- independent human usability testing;
- formal external certification of Claude Code, Codex, or any other hosted runtime;
- universal runtime compatibility;
- perfect AI judgment or guaranteed model behavior;
- operating-system sandboxing or physical confinement of a direct-file runtime;
- PMM Engine qualification, customer outcomes, testimonials, or ROI.

Both shipped runtime profiles remain uncertified unless a specific installed instance later records its own complete certification evidence. Direct native filesystem access remains a compliance boundary rather than an operating-system security boundary.

The public record contains this summary and the compact machine-readable gate. Raw traces, private fixtures, volatile working paths, credentials, and environment-specific artifacts are intentionally excluded.

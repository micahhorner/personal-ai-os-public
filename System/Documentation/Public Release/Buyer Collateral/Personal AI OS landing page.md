# Personal AI OS landing page — v1.64.4

**Eyebrow:** Free and open source · MIT · plain files

## Stop rebuilding your AI's context every time you open a chat.

Personal AI OS gives that context a home you control: one folder for your notes, sources, decisions, preferences, rules, and repeatable procedures.

A supported filesystem-capable AI reads the rules, loads what the task needs, and works against your files. The release ships profiles for Claude Code and Codex. Change the runtime later; keep the folder.

## It is a folder, not another account

- Ordinary Markdown and YAML you can open with a text editor.
- No Personal AI OS database, hosted service, or subscription required.
- Your knowledge layer checks locally against Open Knowledge Format v0.2.
- The core and open shipped packs are MIT-licensed.

## The rules are written down

The system routes requests to written procedures. Supported writers use review plans, confirmation, safe-path checks, validation, and recovery. Consequential changes leave a journal entry. One setting tells compliant tools and runtimes to stop writing.

That is disciplined operation, not magic. In direct-file mode, the runtime can technically open files its OS account can reach. Privacy and write restrictions are compliance controls, not an operating-system sandbox. Never put secrets in the vault.

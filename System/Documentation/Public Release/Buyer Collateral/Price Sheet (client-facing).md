**Everything below installs and runs on your machine, in files you own outright. You bring your own AI subscription (billed by that vendor to you — we never resell AI access or sit between you and your AI bill).**

**Your knowledge layer is stored in an open standard, and you can check that yourself.** Your notes live as ordinary Markdown in an **Open Knowledge Format v0.2** bundle. `aios okf-check` reports the exact folders and records it checked. The system machinery is outside that knowledge-layer verdict; the core itself is MIT-licensed.

**It helps you see the ripples of a change.** For consequential supported changes, the system scans declared relationships, reports affected records, and flags review obligations. It does not decide that every connected note is correct; you or your AI still review the surfaced work.

**It avoids loading the whole vault for every question.** Retrieval starts from compact indexes and opens selected records as needed. Actual cost and speed still depend on the runtime, model, request, and vault; no fixed token or savings claim is part of this offer.

**Security boundary:** filtered retrieval enforces the product's note-level privacy and domain rules. A direct-file AI is not physically sandboxed and can technically open files its OS account can reach. Never store secrets in the vault.

# Flow 8 — Bring Your Own Method & Notes

**If you already organize notes your way, the system learns your method, speaks your language, and can move your existing notes in safely — offered once, never pushed.**

Visual: `System/Documentation/Diagrams/svg/32-flow-bring-your-own-method.svg`.

---

## Your goal

Adopt the system without abandoning how you already think and file — PARA, Zettelkasten, daily notes, or your own invention.

## What you say

> During setup: "Yes, read how my notes are organized."
> "Bring my existing notes in."

## What happens

1. With your permission (read-only), it reads how your existing notes are organized.
2. It drafts a **one-page map of your method** (`80 User/workstyle.md`) — your names, tags, and routines — for you to correct.
3. From then on it uses **your** terms when filing and naming, and treats your "never touch" list as absolutely off-limits.
4. If you bring old notes in, the import runs **dry-run → your approval → apply**, keeps your originals read-only, and drops nothing (anything unmapped is quarantined to the Inbox).

## What you get

- A system that speaks your language and respects your structure.
- Your existing notes moved in safely — or left exactly where they are.

## If it goes wrong

- **Map is wrong?** Edit `80 User/workstyle.md` anytime; delete it and default behavior returns exactly.
- **Not ready to import?** It's offered once during setup and never pushed again unless you raise it.

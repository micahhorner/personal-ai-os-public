# Flows of Work

**This is the map of everything you actually do with AI OS. Eleven flows, grouped by when you'd reach for them. Each has its own step-by-step walkthrough: your goal, what you say, what happens, and what you get. Start at the top; you only need the daily loop to get value.**

See the visual map: `System/Documentation/Diagrams/svg/24-flows-of-work-map.svg`.

---

## Set up · once

1. **Get set up (onboarding)** — a guided conversation that makes the folder yours and sets your privacy defaults. Only the first two stages are needed to start. → `Flows/1 - Get Set Up.md`
8. **Bring your own method & notes** — if you already organize notes your way (PARA, Zettelkasten, daily notes), the AI learns your method and can move your existing notes in. → `Flows/8 - Bring Your Own Method.md`

## The daily loop

This is the rhythm that earns the system its keep — capture, work, record, let it tidy.

2. **Capture & file** — drop anything into the Inbox, say "process my inbox," and it becomes connected, cited knowledge. → `Flows/2 - Capture and File.md`
3. **Ask & get cited answers** — ask in plain language; answers come from your own records, cited by name (or an honest "nothing on that"). → `Flows/3 - Ask and Get Answers.md`
4. **Record a decision** — "record a decision: …" captures what was decided, why, and when to revisit. → `Flows/4 - Record a Decision.md`
5. **Add spoken/tacit context** — tell it something that isn't written down; it's captured as a first-class source, labeled as coming from you. → `Flows/5 - Add Spoken Context.md`
6. **Produce a deliverable** — "draft the X from what we know"; the output lands in `40 Outputs/`, linked to everything it drew on. → `Flows/6 - Produce a Deliverable.md`

## Keep it healthy · weekly

7. **Weekly maintenance** — "run maintenance"; stale items flagged, the Inbox reported (and drained only if you say so), and you get a short list of judgment calls. → `Flows/7 - Weekly Maintenance.md`
10. **Review the AI's work** — skim the journal, check stale flags and approvals; your audit trail in plain terms. → `Flows/10 - Review the AI's Work.md`

## Grow & recover · as needed

9. **Grow the system** — want a new kind of note or a new automation? The Extension Builder walks you through it. → `Flows/9 - Grow the System.md`
11. **Stop & recover** — something looks wrong? One line stops all AI writes; the journal and checkpoints let you restore any earlier state. → `Flows/11 - Stop and Recover.md`

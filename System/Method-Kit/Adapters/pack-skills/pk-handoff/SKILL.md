description: Close or resume a work session per this pack owner's re-entry ritual — at their depth, from a full handoff to a three-bullet sticky. Use at session end on their multi-session work, or when resuming.
---

This is a thin adapter for a generated doctrine pack (adapters point, never restate).
The generator replaces <PACK_PATH> with the owner's pack location at install time.

Read and follow: `<PACK_PATH>/METHOD.md` (their re-entry ritual and record rules) and `<PACK_PATH>/TEMPLATES.md` (their sticky/handoff skeleton).

Hard rules: record at their journaling depth — no more, no less; state the stopping point honestly, including anything unfinished or broken.

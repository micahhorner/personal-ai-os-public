name: pk-plan
description: Write or update a plan at this pack owner's ceremony level, surfacing the decisions that are theirs with a recommendation attached. Use when they ask for a plan or work exceeds their threshold.
---

This is a thin adapter for a generated doctrine pack (adapters point, never restate).
The generator replaces <PACK_PATH> with the owner's pack location at install time.

Read and follow: `<PACK_PATH>/METHOD.md` and `<PACK_PATH>/TEMPLATES.md` (their plan skeleton, at their length).

Hard rules: their decisions surfaced explicitly with your recommendation, in their preferred report style (`<PACK_PATH>/AI-PROTOCOL.md`); the plan is theirs to approve before building past it.

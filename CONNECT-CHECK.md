# Connect Check

**This file proves your AI can see your OS.** Ask your AI: *"read CONNECT-CHECK.md and tell me the token."* If it returns the token below, it is reading your real folder on your computer — not an uploaded copy, and not a stale GitHub mirror.

Token to read back: `AIOS-CONNECTED-4F9K`

That is only the *read* half. To operate your OS, your AI must also be able to **write** to it. `System/Onboarding/Connect Your AI.md` walks you through the second half — your AI writes a scratch file that you confirm appears in Obsidian. Passing both means you are connected and ready to be onboarded.
